Xibei Zhang

Hi! I am a PhD student at Chemistry Department of University College London (UCL). Welcome to my website!

During my PhD at UCL, I ask how fundamental physical principles can clarify biomedicine. My research focuses on drug resistance prediction and drug discovery.

For drug resistance study, I combine molecular dynamics simulations, free-energy calculations, and machine learning to investigate how mutations affect protein–drug interactions. By revealing these mechanisms, I aim to build predictive models that deepen understanding and guide clinical trial decisions. Currently I focus on pyrazinamide and rifampicin, first-line tuberculosis drugs.

For drug discovery study, I actively participate in IMPECCABLE, an integrated drug-discovery project bringing physics-based simulation together with generative AI and experimental insight. My previous training in chemistry, solid mechanics, civil engineering, and geophysics has shaped a curious, interdisciplinary approach to my current scientific problems.
